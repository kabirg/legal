Pulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Last updated: October 30, 2026
Applies to the Pulse iOS app (bundle ID com.pulsebudget.app)

Pulse is a personal and household budgeting app for iPhone, developed and operated by Kabir Gupta, an individual developer based in California, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Pulse collects, how it is used, who it is shared with and the choices you have.

In short: Pulse stores the account, budget and (if you link a bank) transaction data needed to run the app. We do not sell your data, use no advertising or analytics SDKs, and do not track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

Account information

You can sign up with an email address and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google sign-in. We store your email address, display name, an optional profile photo and a unique user ID.

Budget data you enter

Budgets, categories, transactions you add or edit, rules and settings are stored in your account so they sync to your devices and, in household mode, to your household.

Linked bank and card data (optional)

Linking a bank or card account is optional and uses Plaid, a third-party service. Plaid sends us transactions (date, merchant or payee, amount and category), account names, masked account numbers and balances. The Plaid access token is stored encrypted. You can unlink at any time; unlinking removes the connection at Plaid and deletes the token. If your subscription lapses, bank connections are unlinked automatically. Plaid's handling of your data is governed by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Optional push notifications about sync and month-close events are delivered through Apple (APNs); if you turn them on, we store the device token Apple issues. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

Crash reports

Crash reports collected by Apple's MetricKit are uploaded to our backend. They contain technical details (app version, device model, iOS version and the code that crashed) but not your transactions or account data.

What we do not collect

Pulse has no analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you across other apps or websites, and never sells or rents personal information. Subscription payments are handled by Apple; your card details never reach us.

2. How we use information

  • To run the app: storing and syncing your budgets and transactions, categorizing spending, producing reports, keeping household data in sync and sending notifications you opt in to.
  • To provide the AI features described in section 3.
  • To diagnose crashes, answer support requests, protect accounts, prevent abuse and comply with applicable law.

3. AI features

Pulse Pro includes AI features that rely on third-party model providers:

  • The chat assistant and monthly report narratives send the relevant transaction and budget data to Anthropic's API.
  • AI rule suggestions use OpenAI's API.
  • Voice input audio is sent to OpenAI for transcription.

These providers process the data only to return a response. Under their API terms, Anthropic and OpenAI do not use data submitted through their APIs to train their models. Your chat history is stored in your account.

5. Household mode

You can invite another person to form a household. Data shared with a household is visible to every member of it, so only invite people you trust with your financial information.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Delete Account in the app's settings permanently deletes your account and all of its data and unlinks any Plaid connections; you can also request deletion by emailing {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Deleted data is removed from our live systems and purged from backups within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider; Plaid tokens are stored encrypted, and row-level security limits every query to the signed-in user's own data. You can optionally require Face ID or Touch ID to open Pulse; iOS enforces this lock on your device, and your biometric data never leaves it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

Pulse is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information (consistent with COPPA); if you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it. Users aged 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian's consent, and you must be 18 or older to link a bank account (see our Terms of Use).

9. Your choices and rights

  • View and edit your account details in the app.
  • Unlink bank and card accounts at any time.
  • Turn off notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app or by email.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing us from the address on your account.

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and we will not treat you differently for doing so. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the address on your account so we can confirm your identity.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and we will tell you in the app or by email before material changes take effect.
