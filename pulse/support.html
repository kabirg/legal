1. What is Pulse?

Pulse is a personal and household budgeting app for iPhone. You can add transactions by hand or link bank and card accounts through Plaid, sort spending into categories, set monthly budgets and review a report at the end of each month. Household mode lets you invite another person so you can budget together. Pulse Pro adds bank linking and AI features: a chat assistant, written monthly summaries, rule suggestions for auto-sorting transactions, and voice input.

2. Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

New Pulse Pro subscribers get a 7-day free trial. The trial is available once per Apple ID. When the trial ends, your subscription converts to the paid plan you chose ($4.99 per month or $29.99 per year) unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. If you cancel during the trial you keep Pro access until the trial period is over and are not charged.

How do I cancel my Pulse Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel through your Apple ID:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Pulse, then Cancel Subscription.

Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. After cancelling you keep Pro features until the end of the period you have already paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple; you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed but Pro features are locked. How do I restore my purchase?

Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, then open the Pulse Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription will be recognized on any device signed in with that Apple ID.

How do I unlink a bank or card account?

Open the linked accounts section in the app's settings, choose the account and tap Unlink. This removes the connection at Plaid and deletes the stored access token, so Pulse no longer receives new data from that account. If your Pulse Pro subscription lapses, all bank connections are unlinked automatically. You can link again at any time while subscribed.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open settings and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all of its data, and unlinks any Plaid connections. It cannot be undone. If you cannot access the app, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address on your account and we will delete it for you. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings as described above.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address on your account and ask for a data export. We will send you a copy of your account data.

How does household mode work?

From the app's settings you can invite another person to your household. Once they accept, shared budgets and transactions are visible to both of you. Only invite someone you trust with your financial information.

What happens to my data when I use the AI features?

The chat assistant and monthly report narratives send the relevant transaction and budget data to Anthropic's API; rule suggestions and voice transcription use OpenAI's API. These providers return a response and, under their API terms, do not use the data to train their models. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Pulse can send optional notifications about sync and month-close events. Manage them in iPhone Settings > Notifications > Pulse.
