Perks Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Last updated: October 30, 2026
Applies to the Perks iOS app (bundle ID com.perksapp.app)

Perks is a credit-card benefits tracker for iPhone, developed and operated by Kabir Gupta, an individual developer based in California, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Perks collects, how it is used, who it is shared with and the choices you have.

In short: Perks stores your email, the cards you tell us you hold and your benefit check-offs. It never collects card numbers. We do not sell your data, use no advertising or analytics SDKs, and do not track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

Account information

You create a Perks account with an email address and password. We store your email address and a unique user ID.

Information you enter

Perks works from what you tell it: which credit cards you hold (chosen from our card catalog), which benefits and statement credits you have used, card renewal dates and the point valuations you set. We never ask for or collect card numbers, security codes or card login details.

Linked card transactions (optional, Pro)

With Perks Pro you may choose to link a card account through Plaid, a third-party service. We receive transactions from the linked account and use them to suggest which benefits or credits you have used. The Plaid access token is stored encrypted. You can unlink at any time; unlinking removes the connection at Plaid and deletes the token. If your subscription lapses, linked accounts are unlinked automatically. Plaid's own handling of your data is governed by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

Perks has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and never sells or rents personal information. Subscription payments are handled entirely by Apple; your card details never reach us.

2. How we use information

  • To run the app: storing your cards, check-offs, renewal dates and valuations, and syncing them to your devices and to a partner you invite.
  • To provide the Pro features described in section 3.
  • To answer support requests.
  • To protect accounts, prevent abuse and comply with applicable law.

3. Optional Pro features

AI advisor. When you ask the advisor a question, by text or voice, your question and relevant context about your cards and benefits are sent to OpenAI's API. Voice recordings are sent to OpenAI for transcription. OpenAI processes this data to return a response and, under its API terms, does not use data submitted through the API to train its models.

Offers. When you search merchant offers, your search terms are sent to Offer.love's public data API, which returns matching offers. No account data is sent with the search.

5. Partner sharing

You can invite one partner using an invite code. Once connected, shared household data such as cards and benefit usage is visible to both of you, so only invite someone you trust.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all of its data at any time with Delete Account in the app's settings; this also unlinks any Plaid connection. You can also request deletion by emailing {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Plaid tokens are stored encrypted, and row-level security limits every database query to the signed-in user's own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

Perks is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them, consistent with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Users aged 13 to 17 may use Perks only with a parent or guardian's consent, and you must be 18 or older to link a card account (see our Terms of Use).

9. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your cards, check-offs and valuations in the app.
  • Unlink a card account at any time.
  • Delete your account in the app or by email.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing us from the address on your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal information. We extend these choices to all users regardless of location, and we will not treat you differently for exercising them. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}; we may ask you to write from the email address on your account to confirm your identity.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new effective date and, for material changes, let you know in the app or by email before they take effect.
