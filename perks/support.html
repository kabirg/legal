1. What is Perks?

Perks is a credit-card benefits tracker for iPhone. Pick the cards you hold from the catalog and Perks lists the credits and perks that come with them, so you can check off what you have used, see what is still unclaimed and keep an eye on renewal dates and the value of your points. You can invite one partner to share a household. Perks Pro adds optional card linking through Plaid (to suggest which credits you have already used), an AI advisor you can ask by text or voice, and merchant offer search.

2. Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

New Perks Pro subscribers get a 7-day free trial. The trial is available once per Apple ID. When the trial ends, your subscription converts to the paid plan you chose ($2.99 per month or $19.99 per year) unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. If you cancel during the trial you keep Pro access until the trial period is over and are not charged.

How do I cancel my Perks Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel through your Apple ID:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Perks, then Cancel Subscription.

Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. After cancelling you keep Pro features until the end of the period you have already paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple; you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed but Pro features are locked. How do I restore my purchase?

Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, then open the Perks Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription will be recognized on any device signed in with that Apple ID.

How do I unlink a card account?

Open the linked accounts section in the app's settings, choose the account and tap Unlink. This removes the connection at Plaid and deletes the stored access token, so Perks no longer receives transactions from that account. If your Perks Pro subscription lapses, linked accounts are unlinked automatically. You can link again at any time while subscribed.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open settings and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all of its data, and unlinks any Plaid connection. It cannot be undone. If you cannot access the app, email support@perkscredit.app from the email address on your account and we will delete it for you. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings as described above.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email support@perkscredit.app from the email address on your account and ask for a data export. We will send you a copy of your account data.

How do I share Perks with my partner?

From the app's settings, generate an invite code and give it to your partner to enter in their copy of Perks. Once connected, shared household data is visible to both of you. Each account can connect with one partner.

A benefit or credit in the catalog looks wrong. What should I do?

Card issuers change benefits, credits and fees often, and the catalog can fall behind. Always confirm details with your issuer before relying on them. If you spot an error, email us with the card and the correction and we will update the catalog.

What happens to my data when I use the AI advisor?

Your question and relevant context about your cards and benefits are sent to OpenAI's API; voice input is sent to OpenAI for transcription. OpenAI returns a response and, under its API terms, does not use the data to train its models. Offer searches send only your search terms to Offer.love. See the Privacy Policy for details.

3. Contact

Email support@perkscredit.app. Typical response time is two business days. To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the Perks app version (shown in the app's settings);
  • what you were doing and what happened, with screenshots if possible.

Please do not send card numbers, security codes or card login details. We will never ask for them.
